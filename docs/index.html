About the game

Adventures, dungeons and the hunt for artefacts.

Explore dangerous ruins, fight monsters, defeat bosses and find rare treasure. Win gear and magical artefacts for your heroes.

Graphics are simple placeholder shapes for now. The game is built to be played first and polished later.

How to play

The first playable is one compact dungeon: fight through a few monsters, face a boss with a visible attack warning, and take the treasure it guards.

  1. Enter the dungeon with a sword and one special ability, the shockwave.
  2. Move through corridors and rooms. Monsters notice you from a distance, chase you and attack. A few drop a green orb that heals you when you walk over it.
  3. Defeat the regular monsters to open the way to the boss room.
  4. Fight the boss. It has much more health and telegraphs its big attack, so you can dodge it.
  5. Open the chest the boss guarded (E): gold, the Ember Blade and a Boss Sigil go into your stash. Loot dropped by monsters is picked up by walking over it.
  6. Step into the glowing exit to finish the run, see what you won, and play again.

You win

Defeat the boss, collect the loot and leave through the exit.

You lose

If your health reaches zero the run ends. Retry restarts the dungeon.

Playing with others

Up to four heroes can fight through the same dungeon. On the first screen choose Play with others, then host a game (friends on the same network type the address it shows, or use a short code when hosting online), press Ready and the host starts. Monsters get 50% more health for each extra hero; a single hero is unchanged. Each hero keeps its own loot: drops go to the hero that walks over them, and everybody opens the chest for their own stash. A fallen hero stays down and watches the others, and the run is lost if everybody falls. Once the boss is beaten, the run is won when the living heroes have left through the exit, or 45 seconds after the first one did. If the host leaves, the game ends for everybody.

Phones

Android and iOS builds come from the same project. On a phone there is a stick on the left to move, a drag anywhere else to look, and buttons on the right: Attack, Wave (the shockwave), Jump, Use (open the chest) and Run. The places (Dungeon, Hero, Profile) are the tabs along the bottom.

Updates

When a newer release is published on GitHub, the Windows game downloads and installs it by itself the next time it starts, and starts again. You can turn this off in Profile. On Android and iOS the update comes from the store or the release page.

Part of the world of Temnorid

Gear and magical artefacts found here will go to the heroes of Lords, which already send heroes into dungeons for loot.

Today Temnorid Dungeons is a separate game and does not talk to Lords. The first screen offers sign-in with Google, a guest account or playing offline; none is needed to play, and playing alone needs no internet connection. A connection is needed only for playing with others and for updates. Your stash and records are saved on your device.
